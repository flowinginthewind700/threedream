Log

    The solver and the renderer share one GPUDevice, and the publish buffer is copied straight into the GPUBuffer three.js already allocated for the position attribute — no node crosses the bus, and the surface and its wire overlay share that one attribute so a single blit fills both. Constraints are solved in colour batches, never two edges of one colour at once, which is what makes the GPU solve race-free without being bit-reproducible. SoftView never writes the simulation, so replay and training stay independent of this layer; e2e/soft_gpu.spec.ts asserts CPU/GPU parity.
